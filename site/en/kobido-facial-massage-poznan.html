What it is

A massage for the face, not just the body

Kobido is a traditional Japanese facial massage. It combines quick tapping and gliding movements with slower, deeper work on the muscles of the face, neck and décolleté. Most guests describe it as surprisingly relaxing, and many come back for it regularly.

If you clench your jaw or grind your teeth, the buccal massage works on the chewing muscles from inside the mouth, with gloves. The myoplastic massage focuses on facial muscle tension, and our sculptural massage is the studio's own longer ritual. We help you choose at the start of the visit.

FAQ

Facial massage - common questions

What is Kobido?

Kobido is a traditional Japanese facial massage that combines fast, rhythmic movements with slower, deeper work on the face, neck and décolleté. It is relaxing and leaves the face feeling fresh.

Can I wear make-up?

You can come with make-up on - we remove it before the massage. Plan to go home without make-up, or bring your own to put back on.

What is a buccal facial massage?

Part of the massage is done from inside the mouth, wearing gloves, to reach the chewing muscles. It is often chosen by people who clench their jaw.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we speak English. The team speaks basic English, enough to agree on pressure and anything to avoid. WhatsApp in English is the easiest way to book.

Is it suitable for everyone?

Please tell us about recent facial treatments such as fillers or Botox, skin conditions or recent dental work. In some cases we suggest waiting a few weeks.
