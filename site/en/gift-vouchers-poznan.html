FAQ

Gift vouchers - common questions

How long is the voucher valid?

Every voucher is valid for 6 months from the date it is issued. The expiry date is printed on the voucher.

Can I choose any amount?

Yes. The amounts listed are simply the most popular choices. You can also choose a specific massage instead of an amount, and the recipient books it whenever suits them.

Digital or paper - how do I get it?

A digital voucher is a PDF sent by e-mail, ready to print or forward. A paper voucher is prepared for pick-up at the studio at ul. Zeylanda 3/1 in Jeżyce, Poznań.

Can the recipient book in English?

Yes, we speak English. The recipient can book on WhatsApp, by phone or with the online form, and we will reply in English.

How do I pay?

Send the order form and we will contact you to confirm the details and how to pay.
