How it works

Vibration, not hand pressure

Endospheres is a machine-based treatment. The therapist guides a handpiece with a matrix of rotating silicone spheres over the skin; they compress and release the tissue in a fast, even rhythm. This is compressive microvibration - a different kind of work from hands, because the stimulus keeps the same strength and pace for the whole session.

We most often work on the thighs, buttocks, stomach and arms. The treatment is not painful; most people describe it as an intense, rhythmic tingling. The skin may stay red for half an hour or so afterwards, which is a normal reaction to increased blood flow.

FAQ

Endospheres therapy - common questions

What is Endospheres therapy?

A machine-based treatment based on compressive microvibration. A handpiece with a matrix of rotating silicone spheres compresses and releases the tissue in a fast rhythm while it is guided over the skin towards the lymph nodes. So it works on the tissue and on circulation, not by relaxing muscles like a manual massage.

Does it hurt?

It should not. Most people describe it as an intense, rhythmic tingling - strong at times, but not painful. The intensity can be adjusted during the session, so tell us if it becomes unpleasant.

Will Endospheres make me lose weight?

No. It is not a weight-loss method and will not replace a calorie deficit or exercise. It works on tissue and circulation, not on body weight. If someone promises you kilograms after a series of treatments, they are promising too much.

How many sessions do I need?

With this method the series is what counts, which is why we have packages of 6 and 10 sessions of 45 minutes. Exactly how many you need, we decide after the first visit - it depends on the area and on what you come in with.

Can I combine Endospheres with massage?

Yes, one does not rule out the other. Endospheres works differently from manual massage, so the two can complement each other - ideally on separate days, not one straight after the other.

When will I see the first effects of Endospheres?

First effects after the 1st session - most people leave with lighter, less swollen legs. Changes in how the skin and body shape look build up over a series, which is why we have packages of 6 and 10 sessions.
