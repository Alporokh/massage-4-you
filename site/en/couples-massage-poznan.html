What it is

The same massage, at the same time

The room holds two massage tables. Each of you has your own therapist, and they work in the same rhythm, so you relax together and leave together. It does not have to be a romantic couple: friends, a mother and daughter, siblings and colleagues book it too.

The length is shared, but the pressure is not. One of you can have a calm, gentle massage while the other gets firmer work on a stiff neck. Tell us at booking, and each therapist will check with you again at the table.

FAQ

Couples massage - common questions

Are both massages in the same room?

Yes. There are two tables side by side and two therapists working at the same time. Both massages start and finish together.

Can we each have a different massage?

The length has to be the same, because the therapists work in parallel. The pressure and focus are set separately for each of you.

Does it have to be a couple?

No. Friends, family members and colleagues book it too. The name describes the format - two people at once - not the relationship.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we speak English. The team speaks basic English, enough to agree on pressure and any areas to avoid. WhatsApp in English is the easiest way to book.

How far ahead should we book?

A little earlier than for one person, because two therapists need to be free at once. On weekdays a few days is usually enough; for weekends and around Valentine's Day or Christmas, ask about two weeks ahead.
