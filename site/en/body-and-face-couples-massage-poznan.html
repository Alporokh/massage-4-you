What it is

Two treatments in one visit, for both of you

Our longest shared treatment: a relaxing full-body massage followed by a facial massage, without leaving the room and without a second appointment. Two therapists work in parallel at two tables, so both of you go through the same sequence at the same time.

The order matters and is not random. The body comes first, because after a long stretch of relaxation the face responds differently than at the start - the neck is already soft, the breathing calmer, and work on the facial muscles goes deeper with less pressure. This is a treatment for an occasion, not for an ordinary Wednesday.

FAQ

Body and face massage for two - common questions

How long does it really take?

One hundred and twenty or one hundred and forty minutes of treatment, plus a few minutes to talk beforehand and a moment to come round afterwards. Block out almost three hours in your diary and do not plan anything straight after.

What comes first - body or face?

The body. After a long stretch of relaxation the face responds differently: the neck is already soft, and work on the facial muscles goes deeper with less pressure. The other way round works less well.

Is the facial part Kobido?

It is a relaxing facial massage, calmer than Kobido. If you want Kobido specifically, we have a separate Kobido option for two.

Can it be split into two visits?

Yes, but then it is simply a body massage and a separate facial massage, charged separately. The point of this treatment is that the second part starts on an already relaxed body.

How early should we book?

This is the longest treatment we offer and needs two therapists for more than two hours, so there are fewer free slots. For a weekend or before holidays, ask two to three weeks in advance.
