What it is

A steady rhythm the body lets go to

Relaxing massage uses smooth, rhythmic techniques along the line of the muscles - stroking, rubbing and slow kneading. The therapist keeps an even pace and gradually goes deeper where the body is most tense, but never past the point of comfort.

We work with natural, hypoallergenic oils. We agree the scope before we start - it covers the whole body, and the longer options leave time to work calmly on the back, neck and shoulders.

FAQ

Relaxing massage - frequently asked questions

How often should I have a relaxing massage?

To keep the effect, we recommend a visit every 2–3 weeks, and once a week for the first month if the tension is strong.

How is a relaxing massage different from a therapeutic one?

We do the relaxing massage at a calm, even rhythm over the whole body. The therapeutic massage is more targeted - it focuses on a specific problem, tension or pain, and costs 20–40 zł more for the same time.

Does a relaxing massage hurt?

No. We set the pressure individually - you should feel the massage, but it should be comfortable. We always respond to what you tell us.

What should I bring?

Nothing special - we provide towels and oils. Just come a few minutes early so you can get ready calmly.

Can I have a massage when pregnant?

Yes, after a consultation. During pregnancy we use gentler techniques and a suitable position.
