What it is

When you cannot predict the next move

Let us start with what confuses people most often: this is not a massage for two people. Here one person lies on the table and two therapists work on them at the same time. A couples massage is the exact opposite - two therapists, two people, two tables.

The effect comes from something simple. In an ordinary massage the body subconsciously predicts where the hand will appear next and prepares for it slightly. With four hands you cannot follow two sources of movement at once, so the mind simply stops trying. That is why most people calm down faster here, even though the pressure stays the same as with one therapist.

FAQ

Four-hands massage - frequently asked questions

How is a four-hands massage different from a couples massage?

It is the opposite. In a couples massage two therapists work on two people, each at their own table. In a four-hands massage both work on one person, at one table. The names sound similar, but the treatments are completely different.

Why do four hands work differently from two?

In an ordinary massage the body subconsciously predicts where the hand will appear next and prepares for it slightly. With four hands you cannot follow two sources of movement at once, so the mind simply lets go. Most people calm down faster than with one therapist, even though the pressure is the same.

Do both therapists do the same thing?

They work in mirror image and at the same pace - that synchronisation is what creates the effect. One leads the rhythm, the other mirrors it, which is why this treatment is done by therapists who are used to each other and work in tune.

Is an hour enough?

Yes, and that is an advantage of this treatment. Four hands cover twice as much of the body in the same time, so an hour gives a range that would take much longer with one therapist. It is a good choice when you are short of time but want to leave truly relaxed.

How far ahead should I book?

Earlier than for an ordinary massage, because the time has to suit two therapists at once. During the week a few days is usually enough; for weekends it is worth asking a week ahead.
