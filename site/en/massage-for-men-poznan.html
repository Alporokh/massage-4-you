Three massages men come to us for

Men usually come to us for one of three reasons: after training, with a sore back after work, or with tension that has not let go for a long time. Each one calls for a different massage - and it is better to match it to the problem than to whatever sounds strongest.

A sports massage works harder on the areas loaded by exercise. A back and neck massage is shorter and focused on the upper body. A deep tissue massage is slow, precise work on tension that keeps coming back. Not sure which? Tell us on WhatsApp what bothers you and we will suggest one.

FAQ

Massage for men - common questions

Can the massage be really strong?

Yes, we adjust the pressure to you and correct it during the session. Strong does not mean painful, though - if you hold your breath or tense up, the work stops being effective. Just say so and we will change the pace.

Which massage for back pain from desk work?

For the neck, shoulders and upper back, a back and neck massage (30-60 minutes) is usually enough. If the tension has been coming back for months, a deep tissue massage is better, and if your whole body aches, a therapeutic full-body massage.

How do I prepare?

Just arrive a few minutes early. We provide towels and oils. You undress to your underwear and lie covered with a towel - we only uncover the area we are working on.

Does sports massage make sense outside the competition season?

Yes. With regular training, a good rhythm is one visit every two to three weeks, and more often while preparing for a race. Before a competition, leave at least two days after a strong massage.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we speak English. The team speaks basic English, which is plenty for talking about pressure, pain and training. Writing to us on WhatsApp in English works well.
