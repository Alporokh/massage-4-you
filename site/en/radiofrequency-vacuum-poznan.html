How it works

Deep warmth and suction

Radiofrequency (RF) heats the deeper layers of the skin. The warmth prompts the skin to rebuild collagen, the protein responsible for its elasticity. Suction (vacuum) lifts a fold of skin, improves microcirculation and helps spread the heat evenly.

It is a treatment for skin quality, not for the amount of fat. Collagen rebuilds slowly, so the effect builds over a series of treatments and keeps growing for a while after it ends.

Step by step

How the treatment goes

1

Health questions

We ask about pregnancy, pacemakers and other electronic implants, metal in the body, chronic conditions and cancer treatment. With radiofrequency this conversation is mandatory.

2

Choosing the area

We agree what we work on and whether 30 minutes is enough or 60 is better.

3

The treatment

The handpiece moves over the skin, combining warmth with suction. The warmth is clearly noticeable but should not be unpleasant - we keep asking how you feel.

4

Afterwards

The skin may be pink and warm for a short while. It is worth drinking water. If you are planning a series, we agree the rhythm of the next visits straight away.

FAQ

Radiofrequency with vacuum - common questions

What is radiofrequency with vacuum?

A treatment that combines radiofrequency, which heats the deeper layers of the skin, with suction, which lifts the skin and boosts circulation. The warmth prompts the skin to rebuild collagen, so over time it becomes firmer.

Will radiofrequency make me lose weight?

No. It is a treatment for skin quality and tone, not for reducing fat or body weight.

How many treatments do I need?

The effect builds over a series, because collagen rebuilds slowly. We decide the number of treatments and the gaps between them after the first visit.

Does it hurt?

It should not. You feel clear warmth and suction. We adjust the power as we go, asking how you feel.

Can I combine radiofrequency with massage?

Yes. Radiofrequency complements anti-cellulite massage or lymphatic drainage well - ideally on separate days.

When will I see the first effects of radiofrequency with vacuum?

First effects after the 1st session - the skin is warmed, tighter and smoother. A lasting improvement in firmness needs a series, because collagen rebuilds slowly and the effect keeps growing after the series ends.
