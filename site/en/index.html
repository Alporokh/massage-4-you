About us

A quiet massage studio in Jeżyce

Massage 4 You is a small massage studio at ul. Zeylanda 3/1, 60-808 Poznań, in the Jeżyce district. Our therapists have years of experience and adjust every massage to what your body needs that day.

We speak English. The team speaks basic English, which is plenty for talking about pressure, sore spots and anything we should avoid. Many guests from abroad simply write to us on WhatsApp.
