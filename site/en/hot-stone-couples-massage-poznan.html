What it is

The heat does part of the work for the hands

Warm basalt guided over the body and placed along the spine. Warmed tissue lets go faster, so what would need clear pressure in an ordinary massage relaxes almost by itself here. In the version for two, two therapists work in parallel at two tables.

It is the gentlest way to reach deep, and that is why it works so well as a shared visit: you do not need to like strong pressure to leave truly relaxed. In winter it is also chosen by couples who simply find it hard to warm up.

Contraindications

When to postpone your visit

Uncontrolled high blood pressure
Heart and circulation disease
Varicose veins and thrombosis
Diabetes with neuropathy
Inflammation and skin changes
Pregnancy

This list is longer than for a classic massage, because heat is an extra load on the circulation, and with reduced sensation it is harder to judge whether the temperature is safe. Contraindications apply to each person separately - if they apply to only one of you, we can offer the other another option for two.

FAQ

Hot stone massage for two - common questions

Do the stones burn?

No. We heat them in water to a temperature that feels pleasantly hot, not scalding, and test it on each person's back before we begin. If at any point the heat is too much, just say so.

Do we both get the stones?

Yes. Both therapists work with stones in parallel, each at her own table. The treatment starts and ends at the same moment for both of you.

What stone do you use?

Basalt - a volcanic rock that holds heat for a long time and releases it evenly. Larger stones go along the spine, and the therapist works with smaller ones in her hand.

What if one of us has contraindications?

We assess contraindications separately. If heat is ruled out for only one person, the other can have the stones while the first has a relaxing or therapeutic massage at the same time - we then charge for the chosen options.

How early should we book?

Earlier than for a single massage, because the time has to suit two therapists at once. The stones also need extra preparation. On weekdays a few days ahead is usually fine; for weekends and before holidays, ask two weeks in advance.
